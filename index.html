Product Info

What is EQUITON?

EQUITON is a veterinarian and equine nutritionist approved multi-vitamin, mineral & amino acid supplement, formulated to support performance, breeding and convalescence.

Who is it for?
  • Racehorses and performance horses in full training
  • Eventers, showjumpers, dressage and hunting horses
  • Broodmares
  • Yearlings, especially sales preparation
  • Horses recovering from illness or in low condition
  • Horses with low haemoglobin
  • Horses with dull or poor coats
  • Horses travelling to and competing away from home
  • Amateur and leisure horses who deserve the same complete support
What are the withdrawal times for racing and competition?

Withdraw EQUITON 10 hours before competition.
